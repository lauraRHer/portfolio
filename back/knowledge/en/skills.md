## Laura's frontend skills
Laura works with Angular and TypeScript, component libraries such as ng-zorro, PrimeNG and Clarity, Leaflet maps, ECharts and Plotly.js charts, GSAP animations, IFC/BIM models with That Open, translations (i18n) and Figma designs.

## Laura's backend and data skills
Laura works with Python and FastAPI, PostgreSQL databases with PostGIS and GeoJSON data, Celery task queues, data migrations from CSV and JSON, DBeaver and environment management with uv and Poetry. During her internship she also worked with .NET. For security she uses Keycloak (including its setup, administration and two-factor authentication), JWT and cross-application roles and permissions.

## Laura's DevOps and quality skills
Laura works with Git, CI/CD, Docker (containers for frontend and backend), separate development and production environments, SonarQube, Cypress end-to-end tests and pytest, always on Linux and using Scrum. She does not use Jest.

## Artificial intelligence: what Laura has built
At CARTIF, Laura built with a colleague an API that extracts data from receipts and invoices with a local vision model (Ollama). In her personal projects she built a custom RAG with LlamaIndex and this portfolio's assistant (LangGraph, hybrid search, local models and a provider-agnostic architecture). She uses coding agents in her work: ChatGPT's Codex for the past year, with well-scoped tasks and always reviewing the changes, and now Claude Code.

## Laura's AI foundations
Laura has trained in machine learning and deep learning (Universidad de Valladolid) and in generative AI (Universidad Politécnica de Madrid): mathematical foundations, neural networks, backpropagation, embeddings, transformers and attention, model training, PyTorch, pandas, NumPy, Hugging Face, LangChain, LangGraph, Langfuse and RAG. She has a knack for mathematics, which helps her understand in depth how models work.
