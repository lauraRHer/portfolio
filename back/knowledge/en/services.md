## What services Laura offers
Laura can take an application end to end or join a team. She offers: AI-powered applications (data extraction from images and documents with local models, and RAG assistants over your own information); full-stack web applications with Angular and Python APIs; maps and data visualisation (Leaflet, PostGIS, ECharts, Plotly.js); and APIs, data and deployment (FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Keycloak, Docker and CI/CD). She works remotely, either employed or as a freelancer on a fixed commitment.
