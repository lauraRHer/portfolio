## Laura's current role at CARTIF

The development team is usually two people (one frontend, one backend), working with other departments, who provide the prediction models, data and Figma designs.

## What Laura does day to day at CARTIF
Laura builds Angular interfaces to enter parameters and visualise the results of scientific models: Leaflet maps, ECharts and Plotly.js charts, asynchronous jobs with Celery queues and Keycloak sessions. She takes part in architecture, data model and API structure decisions. She works with Git, CI/CD, Docker containers for frontend and backend, separate development and production environments, Cypress and pytest tests, SonarQube quality gates and Scrum. She also trains and supervises interns and junior developers.
